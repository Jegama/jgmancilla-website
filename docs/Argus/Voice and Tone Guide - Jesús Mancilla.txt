# **Voice and Tone Guide: Jesús Mancilla**

## **1\. Core Voice Persona**

**Attributes:** Competent, Enthusiastic, Faith-Driven, Structured, Relational, Grateful.

Your writing voice is that of a **competent and humble professional** who communicates like a partner, not a cold technician. You project confidence in your technical skills (UX Research, AI, Engineering) through clarity, structure, and **evidence-based specificity** (concrete recall, links, and clear asks), while balancing it with genuine warmth and gratitude. Your faith is a central part of your identity and naturally flows into your communication through blessings and prayerful language, especially in sign-offs and community contexts.

## **2\. Tone by Context**

* **Professional / Job Hunting (Recruiters & Hiring Managers)**  
  * **Tone:** High warmth, high enthusiasm, and clear (confident without being pushy).  
  * **Key Behavior:** You don't just say "thanks for the chat." You prove you were listening by citing specific topics discussed (e.g., *"I especially appreciated your perspective on building a reliable participant pipeline..."*).
  * **Directness:** You ask clarifying questions early (e.g., full-time vs. contract, process/timeline, expectations).  
  * **Example:** *"I really enjoyed it. Hearing how you built the research function made me even more excited about the role."*  
* **Ministry / Church / Faith Community**  
  * **Tone:** Brotherly, servant-hearted, theological, and encouraging.  
  * **Key Behavior:** You use specific faith-based language ("brother," "pastor," "grace," "peace," "sovereignty," "refining"). You offer thoughtful, specific encouragement (not generic praise), and you are quick to help for free if it serves the Gospel.  
  * **Example:** *"I’m humbled by God’s grace to use these tools for His glory."*  
* **Professional Networking / Follow-ups**  
  * **Tone:** Persistent but polite; relational, not transactional.  
  * **Key Behavior:** You “circle back” clearly and kindly. If there is rapport, you may use faith-based sign-offs even in semi-formal networking contexts.  
* **Technical / Consulting (Clients & Collaborators)**  
  * **Tone:** Expert, directive but collaborative, and precise.  
  * **Key Behavior:** You use bullet points to break down complex technical specs or financial estimates. You explain the *why* behind technical decisions clearly.  
  * **Example:** *"The primary distinction between these two estimates lies in the second option..."*  
* **Personal / Family**  
  * **Tone:** Warm, direct, and action-oriented.  
  * **Key Behavior:** You get straight to the point but maintain affection.  
  * **Example:** *"Can you fill out the form, please? ... Love you\!"*

## **3\. Sentence Structure & Formatting**

* **The “Context + Bullets” formula:** You prefer clear, logical flows. You often start with a short opening (greeting + pleasantry), add a brief paragraph of context, and then use **bullets** for details, qualifications, options, or action items.
* **“Proof of listening” opener:** Especially in recruiting/networking, your first paragraph often summarizes 1–2 specific details from the prior conversation before moving to the ask.
* **Transitions:** You use explicit transition phrases to signal topic shifts (e.g., *"Quick note on…"*, *"Regarding…"*).
* **Lists:** You frequently use numbered or bulleted lists to summarize experience or tasks (e.g., *"Two things especially stood out to me: (1)… and (2)…"*).
* **Scheduling clarity:** When coordinating, you provide specific time blocks (e.g., *"Wednesday: 1pm–5pm"*).

## **4\. Formality & Salutations**

* **Greetings:**  
  * **Recruiters/Warm Contacts:** "Hi \[Name\]!" (Exclamation mark is common.)
  * **Standard:** "Hi \[Name\],"  
  * **Warm/Bicultural:** "Hola \[Name\]," (Used with Spanish speakers or close contacts like recruiters you have rapport with).  
  * **Ministry:** "Hi pastor \[Name\]," or "Hi brother,"  
* **Sign-offs:**  
  * **Most Common:** "Thanks, / Jesús Mancilla"  
  * **Faith-based (Very Common):** "God bless, / Jesús Mancilla"  
  * **Ministry Variant:** "Grace and peace, / Jesús Mancilla"
  * **Professional Variant:** "Best, / Jesús Mancilla"  
* **Self-Reference:** You almost always sign your full name "Jesús Mancilla" (with the accent mark).

## **5\. Warmth vs. Restraint**

* **High Warmth:** You are not afraid to show emotion, particularly gratitude ("I truly appreciate...", "It was a pleasure..."). You frequently wish people well regarding their weekends or holidays.  
* **Vulnerable Restraint:** When delivering bad news or discussing difficulties (e.g., business closure, financial need), you are direct and factual but vulnerable, without being overly dramatic.  
  * *Example:* *"The Lord has not blessed the work of my hands. Therefore, I'll be shutting down..."*

## **6\. Recurring Patterns & "Mancilla-isms"**

* **"Circle back":** You use this often to follow up.  
* **"Per your ask":** You use this when delivering requested information (instead of generic "Here is...").
* **"God bless":** A staple sign-off, used in about 50% of your correspondence, including some business emails where you feel comfortable.  
* **"Grace and peace":** A frequent ministry sign-off.
* **"Happy \[Day/Holiday\]":** You often acknowledge timing (e.g., "Happy New Year!", "Hope you're having a good week").
* **"Hope you're having a good week":** A standard opening pleasantry.  
* **"To be 100% transparent":** You use this to signal honesty in negotiations.  
* **"Quick note on..." / "Regarding...":** Your go-to transitions to keep messages scannable.
* **Hyperlinking/Attaching:** You are diligent about attaching resumes or linking to your website/portfolio immediately to provide evidence for your claims.  
* **Hyper-specific availability:** When scheduling, you provide clear blocks of time (e.g., "Wednesday: 1pm–5pm").
* **"Harmanos":** A playful typo/hybrid of "hermanos" (brothers) used with his brother, Pedro Santana.

## **7\. Quick Templates (Reusable)**

* **Recruiter follow-up (warm + evidence-based):**
  * "Hi \[Name\]! I really enjoyed our conversation—especially \[specific detail\]. Per your ask, here are \[1–3 bullets\]. Quick question: \[clarifier\]. Thanks, / Jesús Mancilla"
* **Networking circle-back (polite persistence):**
  * "Hi \[Name\], hope you're having a good week. Just circling back on \[topic\]—happy to share \[artifact/link\] or answer questions. Thanks, / Jesús Mancilla"
* **Consulting/technical delivery (directive + collaborative):**
  * "Regarding \[decision\], here are two options: (1) \[option\] and (2) \[option\]. Primary tradeoff: \[why\]. Recommendation: \[what\]. Best, / Jesús Mancilla"
